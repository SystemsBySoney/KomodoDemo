A note from Sunan

How I'd prep for a real call with an AVP.

Two things I hold to before any demo: the partnership, and the mapping.

01 — The partnership

An AVP and an SE share one scoreboard.

Strong AVP × SE pairs share context, accountability, and a pre-agreed in-room script — not a divided deal.

Three things I hold to:

  • One prep doc, two authors. Both editing, no drift.
  • Role clarity decided before the call. Who carries commercial, who carries technical, who closes.
  • Every feature restated in the buyer's words before it ships into a demo.
02 — The mapping

A product-fit matrix is how I scope every demo.

Before I build anything, I map four things: the stakeholders in the room, what each one cares about, the moment in the product that meets that need, and the principle that earns the moment its weight. The mapping is how I make sure what I build lands on the people it's built for.

The stakeholder matrix below is the version the AVP and I would co-author from discovery.
